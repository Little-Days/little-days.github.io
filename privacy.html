Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Little Days is a baby journal that keeps your family’s records on your device. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

Information you enter

You may record your baby’s name, birthday and photo; sleep, feeding, diaper, routine, growth, health and vaccine entries; notes and photos attached to entries; caregiver names; and personal pregnancy notes. In the iPhone app this information is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. In the web version it is stored in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to us.

No analytics, advertising or tracking

Little Days contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party software development kits that collect data. We do not build profiles and we do not track you across apps or websites. The app makes no network requests.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos are used only when you choose to attach a photo. The photo is resized and saved inside your journal on your device.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device for optional sleep reminders.
  • Live Activities show a running sleep timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. They are created on your device.

Backups and exports

Backups and spreadsheets are created only when you ask for them. You choose where they go using the system share sheet, such as Files, iCloud Drive or another app; those destinations are governed by their own privacy policies. Your device’s own backups (for example iCloud Backup) may include the app’s data according to your device settings.

Children’s privacy

Little Days is intended for parents and caregivers and is not directed to children. Information about a child is entered by their caregiver, stays on the caregiver’s device, and is not collected by us.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your journal stays on your device until you erase it. Use Settings → Start over → Erase journal in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Sharing with caregivers

This version does not offer accounts or shared journals. If we add sharing in the future, we will update this policy before that feature is released.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages and sets no cookies. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete support conversations when they are no longer needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Email us at littledays@proton.me. We usually reply within a few days.
