Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Little Days is a baby journal that keeps your family’s records on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

Information you enter

You may record your baby’s name, birthday and photo; sleep, feeding, diaper, routine, growth, health and vaccine entries; notes and photos attached to entries; caregiver names; and personal pregnancy notes. In the iPhone app this information is stored in the app’s private storage on your device and, for journals you keep in iCloud, in your iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit. Entry and profile text is stored in CloudKit’s end-to-end encrypted fields, and photos are encrypted by Apple in iCloud; we have no access to any of it. In the web version it is stored in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to us.

No analytics, advertising or tracking

Little Days contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party software development kits that collect data. We do not build profiles and we do not track you across apps or websites. The app connects only to Apple’s iCloud, for journals you keep there, and to the notification relay described under Sharing with caregivers.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos are used only when you choose to attach a photo. The photo is resized and saved inside your journal on your device.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device for optional sleep reminders.
  • Live Activities show a running sleep timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. In a shared journal, starting or ending a sleep also starts or ends it on the other caregivers’ phones through Apple’s push notification service.

Backups and exports

Backups and spreadsheets are created only when you ask for them. You choose where they go using the system share sheet, such as Files, iCloud Drive or another app; those destinations are governed by their own privacy policies. Your device’s own backups (for example iCloud Backup) may include the app’s data according to your device settings.

Children’s privacy

Little Days is intended for parents and caregivers and is not directed to children. Information about a child is entered by their caregiver, stays on the caregiver’s device or in their iCloud account, and is not collected by us.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your journal stays on your device until you erase it. Use Settings → Start over → Erase journal in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device. A journal kept in iCloud stays in your iCloud account until you delete it with Family → Sharing → Delete from iCloud, which also removes it for the people you shared it with; a caregiver can remove a journal shared with them with Leave this journal. You can also manage the app’s iCloud data in the iOS Settings app.

Sharing with caregivers

You can invite other caregivers to a journal kept in iCloud. Invitations are created and managed with Apple’s iCloud sharing: you choose who to invite and whether they can make changes or only view, and you can remove people or stop sharing at any time. People you invite can see that journal’s profile, entries, photos and caregiver names. Your pregnancy notes, app settings and the name you log as on each phone stay on that phone. There are no Little Days accounts.

To start, update and end the sleep timer on the other caregivers’ phones, the app uses a small relay we run on Cloudflare. It receives a random identifier for the journal and for each phone, Apple push notification tokens, and a sleep’s start time. It never receives names, entries or photos. It keeps the push tokens only while a phone remains part of the journal, and keeps no other records. Apple delivers the notifications, and Apple and Cloudflare process network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages and sets no cookies. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete support conversations when they are no longer needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
