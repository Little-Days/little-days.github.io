Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Email us at littledays@proton.me. We usually reply within a few days.

Where is my journal stored?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud account if you keep the journal in iCloud (needed for sharing). Nothing is uploaded to us, and we can’t read your iCloud data.

How do I invite another caregiver?

Open Family → Sharing → Invite a caregiver, enter the phone number or email of their Apple Account (or choose them from Contacts), and choose whether they can make changes or only view. Little Days opens Messages with an invitation you can edit before sending; the link only works for people you invite. When they open it, Little Days asks who is logging on their phone, and the journal syncs in seconds from then on. Use Manage people to remove someone or stop sharing. Caregivers need an iPhone with iOS 18 or later and an iCloud account.

Will the sleep timer show on everyone’s phone?

Yes. When anyone starts a sleep in a shared journal, the timer appears on the other caregivers’ Lock Screens and Dynamic Islands, and it ends everywhere when anyone ends it, including from the stop button on the Lock Screen.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Journals kept in iCloud appear automatically on a new iPhone signed in to the same iCloud account. You can also open Family → Settings → Backup & export, tap Export and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, then choose Restore with that file on the new phone.

Can each person on a shared phone log under their own name?

Yes, even when several phones use the same Apple ID. In Settings → This iPhone → Logging as, choose who is using this phone. New entries are labeled with that name, and the choice stays on that phone.

Sleep reminders aren’t arriving

Allow notifications in the app under Settings → Sleep reminders, or in the iPhone Settings app under Little Days. Focus modes can silence reminders.

I don’t see the sleep timer on my Lock Screen

Check that Live Activities are on in the iPhone Settings app under Little Days. Very long sleeps may outlast the time iOS keeps a Live Activity on screen.

How do I erase everything?

In the app, open Settings → Start over → Erase journal. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone. To delete a journal from iCloud, and for everyone it’s shared with, use Family → Sharing → Delete from iCloud; to remove a journal someone shared with you, use Leave this journal.

Is Little Days medical advice?

No. Little Days is a personal journal. Talk to your child’s doctor about any health concerns.
